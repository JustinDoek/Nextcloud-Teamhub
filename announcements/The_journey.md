###The Next Chapter for TeamHub
First and foremost, thank you for being a part of the TeamHub community! Thanks to your incredible feedback and rapid adoption, TeamHub has quickly outgrown its original scope as a simple team-based view for Nextcloud.
Although short it’s been an amazing journey so far, and we’re just getting started. We rely on your input to keep improving—if you have feature requests or spot areas for improvement, please continue to log issues on our GitHub.

**A Solid Foundation**
To ensure reliability and security, TeamHub remains tightly integrated with the Nextcloud ecosystem. By adhering to their design, security, and coding standards, we guarantee a secure and future-proof foundation for our users. 
Recently, alongside regular app updates, we’ve also rolled out a completely refreshed brand identity, a new website, and comprehensive documentation.

**What's Next: TeamHub Enterprise**
As TeamHub has grown, we've seen increasing interest from corporate users requiring contracts, production-ready, enterprise-grade capabilities. 
To meet this demand and take the app to the next level, we are pleased to work on TeamHub Enterprise.
This will require us to start a company, develop a business case etc. Exciting times ahead. 

**Our Commitment to the Community**
Our dedication to the open-source Community version remains! We chose an option to keep both versions side-by-side in our app, ensuring long-term stability and sharing new features that benefit all use cases.
To make this seamless, we've introduced a unified licensing backend, meaning both versions are supported within a single app. To gather knowledge about what our users need we created a short feedback form. We would love to read your answers so we can keep building in the right direction. 
Form: https://tldr.host/teamhub/feedback.php 

**Why Upgrade to a License?**
Request a free trial and experience for yourself. You can request one directly from your dashboard: simply navigate to Admin settings -> TeamHub -> License tab.
Upgrading to an Enterprise license directly supports our ongoing development, gives you a contract and unlocks the licensed features for your organization:

Exclusive Functionality: Access advanced features tailored for business workflows.
Custom Branding: Remove TeamHub branding for a seamless, white-label experience.
Direct Developer Support: Benefit from priority assistance and a formal support contract.

**Important Roadmap Updates**
While we are working on these new milestones, standard app development continues. Please take note of these important upcoming technical changes:
_September (1st Release)_ - Nextcloud 32 Deprecation: We will officially drop support for Nextcloud 32. Moving forward, TeamHub will always support the two latest versions of Nextcloud to ensure optimal security and performance.
_October (1st Release)_ - Shared Files Transition: We will retire legacy "shared files" support as Nextcloud Teams transitions toward the Team Folder app. Note: A seamless migration path is already available, allowing team owners to manage both shared and team folders easily.
