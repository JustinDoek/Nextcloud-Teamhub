**Personal layer**
We added a personal layer on top of the TeamHub teams. Often you just want a quick view on what's new or what deserves your attention without going through all the teams you are a member off. 
This is exactly what What's new and My Work bring. communication and work aggregated from the teams in a central personal view. Are you ready to work more efficient? 
Activate a trial license to experience it yourself. When you request one in the next week you get 2 months instead of just one. 
Just go to admin settings - TeamHub - License and request the trial license. 

Check the flyer for more info: https://tldr.host/teamhub/promo/teamhub-personal-layer-onepager.html
